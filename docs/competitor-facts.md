# Competitor facts — sources for /compare/swiftcard-vs-*

Every claim the comparison pages make about a competitor is listed here with the
official page it came from and the day it was checked. The pages read this data
from `src/lib/compare/competitors.ts`; when you update a price there, update the
row here too.

Rules used:

- Only the competitor's own website, help centre, App Store or Google Play
  listing counts as a source. Third-party review and comparison sites were not
  used for any claim.
- "Not listed" means the official pages checked do not state it one way or the
  other. It never means "no".
- Facts are restated in our own words; no competitor marketing copy, logos or
  implied affiliation.
- Several pricing pages (Popl teams, Linq, Dot shop) render with JavaScript or
  are not published; where a number could not be read from an official page it
  is marked "could not verify" and the pages say so.

## SwiftCard facts the pages use (from code, not marketing copy)

| Fact | Where it is enforced |
|---|---|
| Free: 1 card, 2 extra Swift Links, 5 new contacts a month; Pro $4.99/mo or $54/yr (10% off); Office $3.99/seat/mo, 2-seat minimum; 14-day Pro trial | `src/lib/plan.ts` |
| Trial requires a card at checkout; first-time subscribers only | `src/app/api/stripe/checkout/route.ts` |
| Platforms: web, iPhone app, Android app (claimed only where the Play listing is switched on), Apple Watch app | `src/lib/app-store.ts`, `src/lib/brand.ts`, `ios/SwiftCardWatch` |
| Apple Wallet pass; no Google Wallet pass | `src/app/api/wallet/pass/route.ts`, `src/components/AddToWalletButton.tsx` |
| NFC: any writable tag; written from Android Chrome (Web NFC) or with a free NFC app on iPhone; no SwiftCard hardware sold | `src/components/NFCWriter.tsx` |
| Recipient needs no app; save contact + share-back form | `src/app/api/card/[username]/vcard/route.ts`, `src/app/api/leads/route.ts` |
| Leads past the Free cap are kept but locked until upgrade | `LOCKED_LEAD_TAG` in `src/lib/plan.ts` |
| Views: notification per view with source; repeat visits by a known contact notify (`contact_return`); crawlers filtered; 30-minute visit window | `src/lib/push-policy.ts`, `src/lib/card-event-notify.ts` |
| Viewer location is city-level and Pro-only; redacted server-side for Free | `src/lib/location-privacy.ts` |
| Email follow-up on every plan; text follow-up, AI-written follow-ups on Pro; texts to North American (+1) numbers only | `src/lib/plan-content.ts`, `src/lib/lead-followup.ts`, `src/lib/messaging.ts` |
| AI card designer and paper card scanner are Pro | `src/app/api/scan-design/route.ts`, `src/app/api/scanner/route.ts` |
| CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive, Google Contacts, Zapier, CSV export — Pro/Office | `src/components/site/integration-brands.ts`, `src/app/api/leads/export/route.ts` |
| Office: brand and design lock, admin dashboard, contacts stay when a member leaves | `src/app/api/office/brand/route.ts`, `src/app/api/office/members/route.ts` |
| Cancel anytime; downgrade converts design to Free, deletes nothing | `src/lib/plan.ts` (`sanitizeCustomizationForPlan`) |
| Account deletion with a reopen window, then permanent purge | `src/app/account-deleted/page.tsx`, `src/app/api/account/purge-due/route.ts` |
| "We never sell your personal information" | `src/app/privacy/page.tsx` |
